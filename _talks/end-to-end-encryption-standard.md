This session will bring together technical leaders looking to publish a standard (or quasi-standard) for performing end-to-end encryption across various messaging platforms.

Message-level encryption provides a higher grade of security for applications that use shared environments, multi-tenancy, and public cloud services. Currently, there is limited industry collaboration around definition of goals and publishing of specification or guidelines for applications to perform end-to-end encryption across various messaging protocols and platforms.

The goal of this session is to carry over drive-by conversations from MQ Summit 2025 and begin to lay down a roadmap.

**Key Takeaways:**
* Review draft of goals and use cases
* Share a demonstration of use cases
* Review draft points needed
* Identify next steps

**Target Audience:**
* Consensus building technical leaders with interest in publishing a specification (or quasi-specification) for end-to-end message encryption across messaging protocols and platforms.
