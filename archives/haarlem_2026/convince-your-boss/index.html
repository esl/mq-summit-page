Questions your boss might ask

"Can't you just watch the recordings?"

You can: every ticket includes 3 months of exclusive access to the recordings. The labs aren't streamed, though, and neither are the conversations with speakers between sessions.

"Isn't this a vendor event?"

It's organised by Code Sync, not by a broker vendor. Speakers come from competing projects (RabbitMQ, Kafka, IBM MQ, NATS, MQTT, ActiveMQ), so you can compare approaches side by side.

"How big is it?"

The first edition in Berlin (2025) had 350+ attendees across 5 tracks. Small enough to actually talk to people.

"Is there a discount?"

Yes: the promo code MQS2026 takes 20% off at checkout. The prices above already include it.

"Invoices, group tickets?"

Write to info@codesync.global and the team will help.
