Jon has worked for IBM in MQ development since 1999 and has worked on every release of IBM MQ since across a variety of platforms including AIX, OS/400, Linux Intel/PPC/s390x, HP-UX, Solaris SPARC/Itanium, HP NonStop Kernel, MacOS & Windows.

Jons current role is architect for the distributed queue manager and is responsible for MQs data persistence, integrity and transactionality.

Jon still enjoys the deeply technical side of the job, designing and developing new product features and fixes.
