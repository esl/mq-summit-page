Ed Berezitsky is a Streaming Specialist Solutions Architect at AWS, where he helps customers design and implement solutions built on streaming technologies. His focus areas are highly available streaming architectures and disaster recovery for real-time data processing platforms. Before joining AWS, Ed built real-time data processing solutions with Apache NiFi, Kafka, and Spark, and helped customers optimize their high-scale big data systems.
