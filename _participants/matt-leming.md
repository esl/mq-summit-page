Matt Leming is the chief architect for IBM MQ, and a Senior Technical Staff Member. Matt holds a first class bachelor's degree in mathematics from Loughborough and a masters degree in software engineering from Oxford. He is based at IBM Hursley in the UK, where he has worked since 2002. During his tenure at IBM, Matt has spent most of his time working on either IBM MQ, WebSphere Application Server, or both!
